# Likeness on NEAR: hackathon notes

> Draft. The reused-files table and "Built for NEAR" are completed in the docs commit.

## Origin

Likeness on NEAR starts from FaceVisa (GitHub: rohitshukla11/eth_mumbai @ b1976e4), built by Rohit Shukla at
ETHGlobal Mumbai, 5–7 Nov 2026, inside the NEAR HACKATHON 2026 window. The organisers confirmed a fresh repo with
fresh commits. This repo was built in layers, each already in its NEAR form; none of FaceVisa's removed integrations
appear in any commit.

## The demo's real creator

The one real creator is the project's author, Rohit Shukla (`rohitshukla.testnet`). His photo
(`web/public/creators/rohit.jpg`) is a photo of himself that he supplied for this demo on 13 Nov 2026, cropped to
head and shoulders and converted to JPEG (no other edits, no embedded metadata), and published here with his consent.
It is his reference: every ad of him is an edit of it, and its sha256
(`89a4d9973341de96b3342ec2c678ea8e7975b219af30a2b676cfe18883c172af`) is the `capture_sha256` he registers with
(`web/scripts/owner-creator.ts` stores it). There is no creator sign-up; every other account is a brand.

## Synthetic demo creators

Two synthetic demo creators sit beside the one real creator, so the app can be tried with more than one face. They are labelled
"Synthetic demo creator: AI-generated face, not a real person" everywhere they appear (cards, profiles, ads, Verify,
receipts and the standalone verifier). Both faces are our own generated files; no stock photos and no images of
unknown origin.

Maya mirrors the PayPal demo's synthetic creator of the same name (niche, render cap and licence days; price scaled to
NEAR). A third synthetic test account, `likeness-synth-theo.testnet`, was registered and then
paused (`set_active(false)`): the contract has no way to unregister a creator, so it stays paused, takes no requests,
and the app never lists or shortlists it.

Every prompt asked for a fictional adult who does not exist (not a real or famous person). File hashes:
`web/public/synthetic/PROVENANCE.md`. Their accounts are faucet-sponsored testnet accounts, registered with
`web/scripts/seed-synthetic.ts`; decisions for them are made by the operator with those accounts' keys, and labelled
as such (there is no person to consent). The seed stores each face as that account's reference capture, so ads
for them are edits of the synthetic face.

## Video (optional)

Video ads are made with Google Gemini Omni Flash, outside NEAR AI's TEE, only when the server has video turned on and
only for creators who opted in ("Allow video", off by default). Each clip starts from an ad image the creator already
approved, is submitted as a render for the creator's review, and is paid only on their approval. The project's privacy
claim (NEAR AI's private inference) covers images only. Code: `web/lib/video/`.

The homepage's faces (hero, avatars, the "how it works" portrait and the three example cards) are AI-generated by Rohit

`f2a69e0`). They show no real person; the example cards are labelled "EXAMPLE · AI-GENERATED", and none of them is a
creator. List of files: `web/public/landing/PROVENANCE.md`.
